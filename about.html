About Apitory

Apitory is a small, independent studio that builds data APIs and Apify Actors. Everything in the catalog is built around one idea: data should be pulled live from its source at the moment you run it, not served from an index that was built last month and is quietly going stale. When you run an Apitory Actor, the records in your dataset were fetched seconds earlier — from Google Maps, from a company's own career site, from the Companies House register, or from a public Telegram channel.

Buying works the way Apify works. Every purchase, payment, invoice and review happens on Apify itself — we never see your card details, and there is no separate account to create here. Pricing is pay per result: each Actor publishes its rate per 1,000 records, and you pay for what a run actually delivers. There are no subscriptions to monitor and no monthly minimums.

Three principles run through the catalog. Live data only — if a source cannot be fetched at run time, the Actor does not fake freshness. Honest failure — when a search matches nothing or a source refuses a request, the run says so in plain terms instead of returning a silent empty success, and status records are not charged as results. Published prices — the per-1,000 rate is on every page, so the cost of a run is arithmetic you can do before you start.

Apitory is not an agency, and we do not take on bespoke scraping work by email — the catalog is the product. If an Actor is close to what you need but not exact, the support inbox is still the right place to ask.

Support: apitoryhq@gmail.com. We aim to reply within one business day, and your email is answered by the person who built the Actor you are asking about.
